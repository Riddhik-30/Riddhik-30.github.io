Home Irrigation System.

A hands-on STEM project focused on creating a more consistent and adaptable way to water plants using pipes, droppers, and a smart timer.

ROLE Designer
YEAR 2024
FOCUS Smart Irrigation
APPROACH Testing & Iteration
What?

Turning an everyday problem into a practical system.

In 2024, I worked on a home irrigation system using pipes, droppers, and a smart timer. The goal was to create a setup that could provide water to plants more consistently while allowing adjustments based on seasonal and plant needs.

Building the system required more than simply assembling the materials; I had to observe how the system worked, identify what needed to change, and make adjustments to improve its effectiveness.

So What?

Learning through testing and adjustment.

This experience taught me the value of practical problem-solving. I learned that a design does not necessarily work perfectly the first time and that testing and making adjustments are important parts of creating a useful solution.

It also showed me how technology can address everyday problems. Instead of thinking about STEM only as something learned in a classroom, I began to see it as a way to understand real situations and develop solutions that can make everyday tasks more efficient.

Now What?

Carrying a hands-on mindset into computer science.

As a computer science student at UC, I want to continue approaching problems with this hands-on mindset. Whether I am working on software, hardware, or interdisciplinary projects, I want to understand the problem first, test possible solutions, and improve my design based on evidence.

This experience shaped my definition of a Global Citizen Scholar as someone who applies knowledge beyond the classroom to address practical challenges and remains willing to learn, experiment, and improve.
